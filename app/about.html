The Challenge Why حرفة Tunisie

70% of Tunisian artisans still lack digital visibility. Existing tools are fragmented: selling platforms do not support mentorship, funding platforms do not support skills, and training tools are disconnected from real projects.

حرفة Tunisie solves this by unifying collaboration, training, and investment in one dedicated ecosystem built specifically for Tunisian craftsmanship and global reach.
